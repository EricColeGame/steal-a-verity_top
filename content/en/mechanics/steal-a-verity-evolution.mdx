## Frequently Asked Questions About Steal a Verity Evolution

### What is the most effective way to start my steal a verity evolution as a beginner?
Beginners should focus entirely on standard Monster Verity spawns and base treadmill training. Avoid colored monster variants and player vaults until you achieve at least Tier 4 Speed. Consistent low-tier opens allow you to hatch foundational pets, which boost your economy enough to fund rapid mobility upgrades.

### How do I recognize when my speed is high enough to challenge Pink or Winged Verity?
You should only challenge Pink variants when your movement speed comfortably exceeds Tier 8 benchmarks. For Winged Verity, aim for Tier 12+ Speed alongside a comprehensive knowledge of covered map routes. If a monster consistently closes distance on straightaways during standard runs, your speed tier is insufficient for high-tier variants.

### Why do other players catch me even when I have high treadmill stats?
Carrying crates often applies weight penalties or modified movement profiles depending on the crate tier. Furthermore, experienced thieves take optimized pursuit angles rather than chasing directly behind you. Upgrading your raw speed is vital, but mastering tight cornering and breaking line-of-sight is equally critical for evading fast pursuers.

### Should I prioritize pet hatching or treadmill speed early on?
Maintain a balanced 50/50 split during the early game. Raw speed allows you to secure crates safely, but pets supply the economic multipliers needed to afford expensive late-game speed tiers. Hatching a stable roster of income-boosting pets early ensures that your treadmill progression does not stall out at mid-tier thresholds.
