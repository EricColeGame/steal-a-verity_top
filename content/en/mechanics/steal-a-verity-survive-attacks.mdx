## Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, beginners can survive attacks by sticking strictly to standard Monster Verity spawns located near base boundaries. Avoid central arena chokepoints, never attempt to outrun Yellow or Pink variants on base speed, and immediately bank every box you extract to fund early treadmill upgrades.

### What should I do if a faster player chases me while I am carrying a box?
Do not try to outrun a player who clearly possesses higher speed tiers in a straight line. Instead, cut sharp 90-degree corners around terrain obstacles, weave through Monster Verity's aggro perimeter to transfer the monster's attention to your pursuer, or drop into narrow side alleys where raw straight-line speed is less effective.

### Are monster colors just cosmetic, or do they change attack mechanics?
Player reports and community testing confirm that monster colors represent significant threat modifiers. Standard monsters feature predictable pathing, while Yellow, Blue, and Pink variants showcase higher acceleration, longer chase distances, and aggressive lunges. Always adjust your escape routes when colored variants are active.

### Why do I keep losing boxes right outside my base door?
Losing boxes near your entrance usually happens because of bottleneck camping. Lurking players wait near base gates where escaping runners slow down or run out of stamina. To counter this, scout your base perimeter before making your final approach, enter from wide side angles, and make sure your base defenses are upgraded to deter campers.
