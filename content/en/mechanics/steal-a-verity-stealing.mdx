## Frequently Asked Questions About Steal A Verity Stealing

### Why do I keep dying during steal a verity stealing runs even with high speed?
High speed alone does not guarantee a clean extract. Most mid-tier players fail because of poor turning lines and bad abort timing. Sharp 90-degree corners cause deceleration, giving pursuing color monsters or chasing opponents a brief window to close the distance. Practice wide, sweeping arcs around corners to maintain maximum momentum, and abort your run the second an opponent cuts into your primary path.

### How do I tell if a Monster Verity variant is too dangerous to farm?
Watch the monster's aura and skin tint closely before stepping inside the aggro boundary. Yellow variants feature accelerated dash bursts, blue variants boast extended chase durations, and pink variants have relentless tracking that requires significant speed tier advantages. If you have not tested your current speed tier against that specific color variant, downgrade to a standard spawn or let another player trigger the chase first.

### What should I do immediately after pulling off a high-value player heist?
Never stand outside to watch the victim's reaction. Sprint directly to your home deposit zone, open the box immediately to convert it into raw pet assets, and adjust your base defense layout. The player you just robbed—and anyone else in the lobby who saw the drop notice—will likely head straight to your plot looking for quick revenge.

### Is it better to steal Legendary boxes or focus on fast Common boxes?
For pure progression efficiency, chaining fast Common and Rare boxes from Monster Verity is statistically superior until your speed tier allows effortless escapes. Legendary boxes carry immense value, but the extended pursuit timers, heavy weight profiles, and lobby-wide alert banners attract intense competition. Only contest Legendary boxes when the lobby's top-ranked players are AFK or occupied across the map.
