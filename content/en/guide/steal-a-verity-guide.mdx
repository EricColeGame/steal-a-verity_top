## Box Rarities, Variants, and the Winged Verity Event

Boxes are the lifeblood of your operation, but not every crate offers the same risk-to-reward ratio. As detailed later in this **steal a verity guide**, risk assessment is critical when chasing oversized crates across contested terrain.

Monster Verity guards several crate tiers distinguished by their distinct color schemes and dimensions:

*   **Yellow Boxes:** Low-risk beginner containers. They offer quick carry speeds and moderate cash yields, making them ideal for rapid cycling during the early game.
*   **Blue Boxes:** Mid-tier containers with enhanced payout potential. Carrying these slightly lowers your sprint acceleration.
*   **Pink Boxes:** High-tier containers that award premium companion drops. These boxes have a higher mass penalty and require clear exit routes.
*   **Legendary & Oversized Boxes:** Massive crates that signal extreme value. Snatching one alerts nearby players, turning your return sprint into a target for the whole lobby.

The community recognizes three essential Speed gates:

### The 10,000 Speed Gate (Early Mobility)
Reaching 10k Speed frees you from the immediate clutches of Monster Verity. At this tier, standard Yellow and Blue crates no longer induce enough drag to let neutral threats catch you. You can easily outrun rookie players who have not committed their income to treadmill workouts.

At 50k Speed, you gain enough agility to attempt Pink and Oversized crate runs comfortably. You can quickly skirt around base perimeters, feint approaching attackers, and recover dropped crates before rival opportunists can react.

Crossing the approximate 1 Million Speed threshold unlocks high-tier world transitions and turns your character into an untouchable blur. At this velocity, base-to-center travel takes mere seconds, making you nearly impossible to tackle unless you run straight into a stationary trap.

## Base Defense and Counter-Raid Strategies

Your base is your vault, but until you fortify its boundaries, it functions as a public storehouse for aggressive neighbors. A common mistake highlighted across our **steal a verity guide** sessions is leaving base gates unfortified while farming. If you spend ten minutes securing high-value crates only to have a rival slip in and swipe your active income generators, your progress stalls out.

```
       [Outer Perimeter]  --> Visual deterrents & early warning zone
              │
       [Shield Gates]     --> Primary barrier against high-speed sprinters
              │
       [Display Core]     --> Deepest interior pocket for highest-tier pets
```

Defending your base requires a combination of architectural security and map awareness:

1.  **Keep Shields Active:** Always maintain your barrier integrity before embarking on long farming expeditions. A powered gate halts high-speed sprinters in their tracks.
2.  **Stagger Your Display Layout:** Do not place your highest-earning companions near the front entrance. Push your most lucrative units deep into the back corner of your plot to force raiders to spend more time inside your territory.
3.  **Punish Overextended Raiders:** If a player enters your base, do not panic. Use the interior boundary collision mechanics to corner them while their carrying penalty slows their escape.
4.  **Bide Your Counter-Attacks:** Never blind-raid an active base. Wait until an opponent commits to a distant Monster Verity run or engages another player before slipping into their yard to grab unprotected assets.
