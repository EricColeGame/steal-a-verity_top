## The Core Loop: Train, Retest, and Farm

Blindly running on the treadmill without validating your actual performance creates a dangerous sense of false security. Reaching top-tier speed requires adopting a disciplined three-phase gameplay loop: **Train, Retest, and Farm**.

```
   [ 1. TRAIN ] ──> Spend earnings on treadmill & build raw Speed
         ▲                                                │
         │                                                ▼
   [ 3. FARM ]  <── Validate times on fixed lines <── [ 2. RETEST ]
   Secure higher-tier
   chests & boost pets
```

1. **Train**: Convert a saved block of cash into Speed points on your upgraded treadmill.
2. **Retest**: Step off the machine and execute a standardized benchmark run. Pick a specific route—such as departing your gate, grabbing a designated Monster Verity chest, and returning—to assess real-world transit time.
3. **Farm**: If the route feels noticeably faster and safer, farm that route repeatedly to build your next cash block. If you still struggle or take monster damage, halt your ambition and continue training before targeting higher chests.

| Benchmark Route | Recommended Speed | Target Hazards | Pass Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Green / Safe Run** | 1,000 – 5,000 | Basic map clutter, long distances | No stamina panic, clean extract under 45s |
| **Color Monster Run** | 10,000 – 25,000 | Roving pathfinder monsters, light choke points | Clean jukes without defensive emergency items |
| **Contested Core Run** | 50,000 – 100,000 | Player thieves, high monster density | Surviving a direct pursuit without dropping cargo |
| **Mythic / Deep Vault** | 500,000+ | Fast server chasers, maximum travel distance | Sub-30-second out-and-back execution |

Keeping a standard benchmark eliminates guesswork. When an exit route that used to induce panic suddenly feels effortless, you know your treadmill investments are paying dividends.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the steal a verity max level for Speed?
Steal A Verity does not enforce a rigid, numbered level cap on characters. However, community consensus views reaching **roughly 1,000,000 (1M) Speed** as the functional **steal a verity max level**. At this threshold, your character can outrun virtually all PvE hazards and cross the server map in seconds, making further upgrades yield diminishing practical returns.

### How do I protect my pet income while training?
Always invest in base door and barrier upgrades before committing to extensive treadmill sessions. If high-ranking thieves frequent your lobby, bank your high-value pets in secure vault slots or train in shorter bursts between active heist rounds so you can actively defend your perimeter.

### Why do I keep getting caught by monsters despite high Speed?
Raw Speed means nothing without pathing discipline. If you turn corners too sharply or sprint straight into environmental choke points, monsters can intercept your hitbox regardless of your stats. Use the **Train → Retest → Farm** cycle to practice obstacle evasion whenever you step up to a faster treadmill tier.

### Do rare pets increase my running speed directly?
No. Pets increase your passive income generation, which is used to purchase treadmill upgrades. The treadmill itself is what permanently increases your Speed stat. Pets provide the fuel; the treadmill provides the engine.
