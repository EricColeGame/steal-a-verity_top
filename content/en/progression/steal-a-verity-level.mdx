## The Train, Retest, and Farm Routine

Mindless AFK training without practical testing creates false confidence. Stepping onto the treadmill for an hour only helps if that gained speed translates into higher carry success rates. To effectively advance your **steal a verity level**, adopt a three-phase operational cycle: Train, Retest, and Farm.

```
[ Train ] ---> Spend banked cash to boost treadmill & run stats
   ^                                                           |
   |                                                           v
[ Farm ]  <--- Validate upgrades on fixed routes <---  [ Retest ]
```

### 1. Train
Bank your pet income and commit a dedicated block of time to the treadmill. Focus entirely on pushing past your next checkpoint rather than checking the lobby scoreboard.

### 2. Retest
Never assume a treadmill upgrade instantly fixes your survival rate. Establish a standard benchmark route—such as a specific Monster Verity approach line—and run it twice: once empty-handed to check route clearance times, and once while carrying a weighted box. 

| Benchmark Test Stage | Route Condition | Passing Criteria | Action on Failure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage A: Sprint Test** | Empty-handed | Cross contested mid-line without slowing down or clipping corners. | Adjust movement lines and camera angles. |
| **Stage B: Hazard Run** | Empty-handed | Bypass color monster aggro radius without burning emergency stamina. | Return to treadmill for an additional speed tier. |
| **Stage C: Box Carry** | Carrying Tier-Appropriate Box | Return box to base vault without dropping cargo during exit encounters. | Strengthen base defense; farm lower-tier boxes first. |

### 3. Farm
If your retest confirms that your benchmark run is cleaner and safer than yesterday, commit to a farming block. Collect your target boxes, hatch improved pets, and prune your roster using a strict value filter. Once your wallet fills up, repeat the cycle.
