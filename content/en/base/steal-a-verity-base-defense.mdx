## The "Defense, Speed, Greed" Spending Hierarchy

Balancing your hard-earned currency between pad upgrades, treadmill training, and new boxes requires strict financial discipline. Investing too heavily in pure defense early on stunts your character's movement, making it impossible to chase down thieves or snatch distant boxes. Conversely, funneling all your coins into Speed leaves your home empty and defenseless.

The optimal upgrade philosophy follows three distinct phases: **enough defense, then Speed, then greed**. 

First, secure your base to stop casual walk-ins. Next, pump your earnings into treadmill training so you have the velocity to contest invaders and escape hostile zones. Finally, once your safety baseline and athletic speed are secured, indulge in greed by expanding pet slots and hunting luxury box spawns.

*   **Post-Heist Retaliation:** Immediately after successfully robbing a rival, harden your pad—they will almost certainly look for immediate revenge.
*   **Speed Checkpoints:** When you cross major speed milestones (such as 10k, 50k, or late-game velocity tiers), re-evaluate whether your defensive setup matches your newly elevated lobby profile.

### Visual Architecture: Show Pets vs. Bank Pets

How you arrange your pad teaches visitors what they can gain from raiding you. Flashing your highest-value pets directly at the boundary line might stroke your ego, but it functions like a neon sign for opportunistic sprinters. 

*   **Bank Pets:** Keep your core revenue drivers positioned as far inward as possible, shielded by physical boundaries and directional chokepoints.
*   **Show Pets:** If you want to show off achievements, use mid-tier trophies or utility companions as bait near outer walkways.
*   **Surplus Management:** Avoid leaving your entire net worth deployed when you are away from the keyboard. Keep your active earning roster compact and manageable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important upgrade for steal a verity base defense?
The most critical upgrade for early **steal a verity base defense** is basic physical access blocking. Stopping opponents from casually walking onto your platform forces them to commit to risky jumps or specialized approaches, giving you adequate reaction time to intercept them before they grab your pets.

### Should I prioritize base defense or treadmill speed first?
Maintain a balanced loop: secure enough base defense to prevent drive-by thefts, then invest heavily into treadmill speed. Having high movement speed allows you to contest thieves, retrieve stolen boxes, and return to your pad quickly when an intruder triggers your defenses.

### How do I protect my pets while AFK training on the treadmill?
To safeguard your pets while training, position your camera to face the primary entrance lane of your base. Keep your highest-earning pets positioned deep within your layout behind your most advanced defensive barriers, rather than displaying them along the perimeter.

### Does a strong base defense completely stop all raids?
No defense prevents 100% of raid attempts. The true metric of a successful setup is reducing the number of clean, uncontested thefts per hour, slowing down invaders enough for you to punish them, and ensuring your passive income remains stable during farming excursions.
